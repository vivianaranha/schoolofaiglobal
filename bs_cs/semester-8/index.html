Semester Theme

This overview page helps learners understand how the subjects fit together.

Synthesize advanced technical work with leadership, ethics, deployment, and career readiness.

Learning Goals

  • Design and evaluate real-world generative AI and responsible-computing solutions.
  • Understand product strategy, entrepreneurship, deployment, and enterprise architecture trade-offs.
  • Complete internship, capstone, and portfolio work that demonstrates professional readiness.
  • Graduate with a coherent story about technical depth, ethics, teamwork, and impact.
