  • Implement metrics, kpis, and experiment design within realistic technology leadership and product management scenarios.

Module 1

Product roles and cross-functional leadership

Technology Leadership and Product Management uses product roles and cross-functional leadership as a building block for more advanced study. Start by understanding the plain-language purpose of the idea, the vocabulary attached to it, and the kinds of questions it helps us answer. In beginner-friendly terms, this module asks: what problem does this concept solve, and why would a computing professional care about it?

At a deeper level, we connect product roles and cross-functional leadership to the broader leadership and ethics pathway. We examine the assumptions behind the concept, how it interacts with adjacent topics in Semester 8: Capstone, Leadership, and Emerging Technologies, and what changes when systems become larger, faster, more distributed, or more safety-critical. Strong students should look for trade-offs, limits, and edge cases rather than memorizing a single rule.

In practice, teams apply product roles and cross-functional leadership while building or improving a student information platform. The value of the topic becomes obvious when design choices need to be justified, tested, documented, and communicated to collaborators.
