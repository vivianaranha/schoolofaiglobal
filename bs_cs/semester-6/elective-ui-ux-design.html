Module 1

User needs, context, and product goals

Elective: UI/UX Design for Software Systems uses user needs, context, and product goals as a building block for more advanced study. Start by understanding the plain-language purpose of the idea, the vocabulary attached to it, and the kinds of questions it helps us answer. In beginner-friendly terms, this module asks: what problem does this concept solve, and why would a computing professional care about it?

At a deeper level, we connect user needs, context, and product goals to the broader leadership and ethics pathway. We examine the assumptions behind the concept, how it interacts with adjacent topics in Semester 6: Data, Security, and Scalable Applications, and what changes when systems become larger, faster, more distributed, or more safety-critical. Strong students should look for trade-offs, limits, and edge cases rather than memorizing a single rule.

In practice, teams apply user needs, context, and product goals while building or improving a student information platform. The value of the topic becomes obvious when design choices need to be justified, tested, documented, and communicated to collaborators.
