Module 1

Understanding the client context and business problem

Industry Project I uses understanding the client context and business problem as a building block for more advanced study. Start by understanding the plain-language purpose of the idea, the vocabulary attached to it, and the kinds of questions it helps us answer. In beginner-friendly terms, this module asks: what problem does this concept solve, and why would a computing professional care about it?

At a deeper level, we connect understanding the client context and business problem to the broader software engineering pathway. We examine the assumptions behind the concept, how it interacts with adjacent topics in Semester 6: Data, Security, and Scalable Applications, and what changes when systems become larger, faster, more distributed, or more safety-critical. Strong students should look for trade-offs, limits, and edge cases rather than memorizing a single rule.

In practice, teams apply understanding the client context and business problem while building or improving a student information platform. The value of the topic becomes obvious when design choices need to be justified, tested, documented, and communicated to collaborators.
