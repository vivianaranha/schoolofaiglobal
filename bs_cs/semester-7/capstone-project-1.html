Module 1

Capstone topic discovery and problem statements

Capstone Project I: Proposal, Design, and Prototype uses capstone topic discovery and problem statements as a building block for more advanced study. Start by understanding the plain-language purpose of the idea, the vocabulary attached to it, and the kinds of questions it helps us answer. In beginner-friendly terms, this module asks: what problem does this concept solve, and why would a computing professional care about it?

At a deeper level, we connect capstone topic discovery and problem statements to the broader research and inquiry pathway.
