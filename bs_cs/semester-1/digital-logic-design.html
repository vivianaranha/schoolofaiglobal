Learning Outcomes

By the end of this subject, a self-paced learner should be able to do the following with confidence.

  • Explain binary numbers and arithmetic operations within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Apply logic gates and truth-functional behavior within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Analyze boolean algebra and identities within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Design truth tables and logical equivalence within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Evaluate karnaugh maps and expression simplification within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Interpret combinational circuit design methodology within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Implement adders, subtractors, and arithmetic units within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Compare multiplexers, decoders, encoders, and selectors within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Troubleshoot flip-flops, registers, and sequential memory within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
  • Connect counters, timing concepts, and digital memory basics within realistic digital logic design scenarios.
