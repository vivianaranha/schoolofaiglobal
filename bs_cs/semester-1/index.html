Semester Theme

This overview page helps learners understand how the subjects fit together.

Build computational thinking, mathematical confidence, and a first-principles understanding of computers.

Learning Goals

  • Understand what computer science covers, from hardware to software and society.
  • Write first programs, reason about logic, and model simple computational solutions.
  • Develop mathematical fluency for binary systems, algebra, and structured problem solving.
  • Practice communication and lab habits expected in technical study.
